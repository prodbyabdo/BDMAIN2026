## Industry-Specific Guardrails

### Healthcare / DME / CGM / Specialty Campaigns
Position services strictly as BPO support, administrative execution, and process scalability.

- **Focus on:** Clean data, process discipline, compliance alignment, scalability.
- **Never promise:** Medical eligibility, coverage, approvals, prescriptions, clinical outcomes.
- **Existing Certifications:** ISO 27001, ISO 9001.
- **Explicitly flag:** When additional certifications (e.g., PCI, SOC 2) may be required. Never promise certifications not in place.

### Cross-Industry Outreach
- Do not explicitly reference DME/CGM campaigns when approaching non-medical prospects.
- Reframe value propositions appropriately for each industry.

### Solar Campaign Rule
- Solar campaigns are **pay-per-hour**.
- **Do NOT** mention pricing models or hourly rates in initial emails or calls.
- Only disclose pricing after explicit interest is confirmed.
